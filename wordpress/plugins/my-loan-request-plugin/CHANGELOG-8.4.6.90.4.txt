8.4.6.90.4 - independent legal paragraphs
- Split run-on clauses in rendered HTML at bold Persian or Latin numbered clause labels (e.g. تبصره ۲ and ۶-۲), preserving the original content.
- Apply the same display-only normalizer to contract views for the applicant, internal expert, public signed viewer, review and reissue previews.
- Never change canonical contract payload, saved signature evidence or hash/digest as a side effect of the formatting repair.
- Ensure semantic paragraphs remain separate in preview and isolated A4 printing.
