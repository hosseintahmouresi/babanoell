8.4.6.78 - Deferred OTP for contract reissue

- Reissue/save now sends only the secure contract preview link to the applicant.
- No OTP is generated, stored, or sent when the administrator creates or resends a reissued contract link.
- The OTP is generated only after the applicant opens the public contract link, reviews the contract, accepts it, and explicitly requests the verification code.
- Reissue public OTP requests do not resend the contract link, avoiding duplicate SMS messages.
- Existing normal contract-signing behavior remains backward-compatible.
- Reissue admin UI wording now clearly distinguishes preview-link delivery from OTP delivery.
- Preview-link delivery is recorded on the active reissue revision for auditability.
